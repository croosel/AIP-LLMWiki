Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order on flowzen.co, we collect the following information necessary to fulfill your order:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, shipping address, and phone number.
  • Payment Information: Credit card details are processed securely by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not store your full credit card number on our servers.
  • Order Information: Products purchased, order value, and shipping preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Communicating with you about your order status
  • Sending shipping confirmations and tracking information
  • Responding to your customer service inquiries
  • Improving our website and customer experience

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Stripe handles your payment information according to their privacy policy. We never have access to your full credit card details.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website. GA4 uses first-party cookies to collect anonymous usage data including page views, session duration, and device type. No personally identifiable information is sent to Google Analytics. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Retention

We retain your order information for as long as necessary to fulfill your order and comply with legal obligations (typically 7 years for tax purposes). You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@flowzen.co.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@flowzen.co.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
