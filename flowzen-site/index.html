The workflow

A 5-minute protocol for midday burnout.

Step 01

Flip the sand

Turn the chronometer. Pour freshly boiled water into the vessel. For the next five minutes, you are offline.

Step 02

Wake the leaves

Drop whole-leaf tea into the warm basin. Smell the roasted aromatics — your olfactory anchor to the present moment.

Step 03

Breathe with the pour

Grip the solid walnut handle. Pour in one smooth, continuous motion. Focus entirely on the sound of liquid meeting clay.

Step 04

Sip and reboot

Three slow, deliberate sips. When the last grain falls, your break is complete — return calibrated.
